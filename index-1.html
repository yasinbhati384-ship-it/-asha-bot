How to use Asha Bot

1. Open your trading chart in another tab and choose the same asset.

2. Set the five buttons on the right to match the five most recent closed candles: green = up, red = down.

3. Press GET SIGNAL. If the pattern is mixed, the bot may return WAIT instead of forcing a trade.

Signal logic

The tool weights recent candle directions more heavily. Strong agreement may show UP or DOWN; mixed patterns show WAIT. It does not calculate real probabilities or know the next candle.
